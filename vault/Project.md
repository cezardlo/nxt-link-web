# Project — what NXT//LINK is

**NXT//LINK** is a bilingual (EN/ES) **B2B industrial marketplace** for the
**El Paso–Juárez Borderplex**.

- **Buyers** = warehouses / manufacturers looking for equipment, products,
  technology, or services.
- **Vendors** = companies that sell those things.
- **NXT//LINK** connects them and earns a **commission** on closed deals.
- It also helps buyers/vendors make contact and get help operating in the
  border region.

## Positioning (Cesar, 2026-07-20 — the north star)
- **Amazon**: search, compare, and purchase products.
- **Fiverr**: find and communicate with service providers.
- **Alibaba**: connect buyers with suppliers, request quotes, handle larger
  business deals.
- **NXT//LINK = all three combined**, then adds **contracts, alerts,
  documents, communication, and long-term relationship tracking**.

The last five are the moat: a buyer whose contracts, files, message history,
and reorder habits live here doesn't leave. Every feature decision should
strengthen at least one of them.

## Product operating model (Cesar, clarified 2026-07-22)

NXT//LINK is not only a directory or RFQ form. It is the end-to-end industrial
purchasing workspace: **find it → evaluate it → purchase it → track everything
in one place.** The central object is a **Project**, which keeps the buyer,
vendors, offering(s), messages, questions, NDAs, documents, demos, pilots,
quotes, decisions, purchase, delivery/implementation, and history together.

The journey flexes to the purchase:
- Simple product: discover → buy now → track delivery → reorder.
- Configured product/service: discover → question/contact sales → RFQ → quote
  revisions → accept → purchase → delivery or implementation.
- Complex technology: create project → NDA/information exchange → demo → pilot
  → compare quotes → contract → purchase → implementation/milestones.

Every listing exposes only the actions that fit it: **Buy now · Request a
quote · Ask a question · Contact sales · Request a demo · Request a pilot ·
Add to project · Save · Compare.** These are entry points into one shared
project/conversation/deal model, not separate disconnected systems.

## Role workspaces

- **Buyer:** personalized discovery, saved vendors/offerings, projects,
  persistent conversations, documents/NDAs, demos/pilots, quote comparison,
  purchases/orders, delivery/implementation tracking, history, and reorder.
- **Vendor:** editable storefront/profile; product/service/technology listings;
  questions, messages, leads, demos/pilots, quote creation/revisions, orders,
  fulfillment/implementation, documents, notifications, analytics, commission
  and payout visibility, plus "View as buyer."
- **Operator:** vendor approval/verification, listing moderation, assisted
  matching, exception handling, deal oversight, and the single commission
  ledger. The operator helps early transactions without becoming a required
  bottleneck for every healthy deal.
- A company may be both buyer and vendor and should switch modes without a
  second account.

## Onboarding contract

Joining must be fast, friendly, bilingual, and progressively personalized.
Account creation asks only name, work email, company, role (buyer/vendor/both),
and language. Then a short, visual, skippable setup asks industry, company type,
location/service area, categories bought or sold, and what matters most (price,
speed, quality, local support, certifications, or cross-border capability).
Save progress automatically and show useful personalized results immediately;
do not block account creation behind a long corporate form. Vendors progressively
add listings, media, specifications, brochures, certifications, service areas,
demo/pilot options, and fulfillment details.

## Revenue contract

- Browsing, saving, asking questions, contacting sales, RFQs, demos, and pilots
  are not themselves the billable event.
- NXT//LINK earns its transparent success commission when an introduced direct
  purchase, accepted quote, demo/pilot conversion, contact-sales deal, or repeat
  transaction becomes completed business through the platform.
- Every completed transaction enters the one ledger with purchase amount,
  NXT//LINK fee, processing cost, vendor payout, refunds/disputes, and status.
- The fee engine remains the only commission-math authority. Product copy must
  never promise a promotion or payment protection the current policy cannot honor.

## Design rules
- **No dark patterns.** No countdown timers / fake scarcity. Static dates only.
- Bilingual everywhere (EN/ES toggle).
- Make comparing easy: compare tables with **fill bars** (price / timeline).
- Vendors can preview "View as a buyer" (like LinkedIn's view-as).
