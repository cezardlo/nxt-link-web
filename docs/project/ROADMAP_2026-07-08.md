## Executive decision

NXT Link is one two-sided industrial marketplace web app for vendors, service
providers, buyers, and NXT Link operators.

Vendors list standardized products, services, bundles, rentals, and pilot
programs. Credential tokens and maturity status determine what they may
publish. Buyers browse, filter, compare, request estimates or quotes, contact
vendors, or ask an NXT Link advisor. Complex purchases proceed through demos,
site assessments, measured pilots, final quotes, purchases, implementation,
and support. NXT Link earns a disclosed fee on protected purchases.

Two paths are part of the same product:

1. **Self-service:** browse -> compare -> contact/quote -> demo/pilot -> purchase.
2. **NXT-assisted:** describe need -> matched shortlist -> comparable quotes -> advisor-coordinated demo/pilot -> purchase.

## P0 — Establish safe data truth

- Preserve current marketplace work and keep MVP work on a dedicated branch.
- Treat the existing Supabase project as live data; no migration or data mutation without explicit approval.
- Decide whether to create a staging database or accept tightly controlled demo-flagged records in the shared database.
- Define one canonical taxonomy for industries, problems/use cases, offering categories, company sizes, facilities, regions, buying methods, and vendor maturity.
- Reconcile vendor-application data into `vendor_profiles`.
- Design credential-token, evidence, expiry, and permission records.
- Repair the unapplied quote/deal schema so it uses `vendor_profiles` and does not collide with the existing intel-era `vendors` table.

Exit: one reviewed model supports vendor -> offering -> inquiry -> quote -> pilot -> purchase without duplicate identities or unsafe migrations.

## P1 — Standardize and verify marketplace supply

- Add startup/emerging, established, service-provider, manufacturer, distributor, integrator, consultant, and hybrid maturity types.
- Build the complete Vendor Fit Profile: industries, problems, company size, facilities, geography, budget, timeline, integrations, decision roles, and not-a-fit conditions.
- Build evidence-backed credential tokens with reviewer, scope, status, and expiration.
- Enforce category-based permissions on the server and database; expired or incomplete vendors cannot bypass publishing through the API.
- Complete standardized product and service fields, packages, case studies, independent testing, references, and startup disclosures.
- Connect products, services, bundles, rentals, and pilots.

Exit: a startup and an established vendor can publish honest, clearly different profiles; buyers can see exactly what NXT Link checked.

## P2 — Complete buyer discovery and contact

- Add buyer company profiles, saved listings, shortlists, and dashboard.
- Filter by industry, problem, budget, company size, facility, location, timeline, pilot, maturity, credentials, compatibility, and buying method.
- Compare up to five offerings using price, delivery, installation, training, warranty, support, maintenance, credentials, missing data, and total cost.
- Add deterministic estimates where vendor price rules allow them; distinguish estimate, vendor quote, and final price.
- Add structured quote requests and an “Ask an NXT Link advisor” path.
- Add buyer-vendor messaging, attachments, demo/call/site-visit scheduling, notifications, moderation, and retention rules.

Exit: a buyer can find three relevant offerings, understand the differences, contact a vendor or advisor, and track the interaction from their dashboard.

## P3 — Complete quote, pilot, and purchase workflow

- Connect marketplace inquiries and NXT-assisted requests to one opportunity model.
- Persist anonymized/versioned RFQ packets where confidentiality is requested.
- Record buyer approval and selected vendor assignments.
- Store structured, versioned vendor quotes and buyer comparison/revisions.
- Build demo/site-visit and pilot planning, responsibilities, milestones, measurements, results, and exit decisions.
- Enforce and log consent, NDA/MNDA/NCA, reveal, and fee acknowledgments.
- Record final selection, purchase value, PO/invoice references, implementation, training, acceptance, support, and closed/lost outcomes.
- Add route authorization, RLS isolation, and end-to-end persona tests.

Exit: one real concierge transaction completes without spreadsheets, hidden database edits, or privacy/fee-gate bypasses.

## P4 — Route payments and scale safely

- Obtain legal/accounting decisions on seller of record, fee payer, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, warranties, cross-border obligations, and payout timing.
- Use a compliant marketplace payment provider; do not manually custody funds or claim escrow without the proper provider and legal structure.
- Support ACH/invoice workflows appropriate for large industrial purchases.
- Reconcile buyer payment, NXT Link fee, vendor payout, refunds, adjustments, and disputes.
- Add verified-engagement reviews, subscriptions, sponsored listings, financing, integrations, and broader categories only after transaction proof.

Exit: success, failure, refund, cancellation, dispute, and payout scenarios are auditable and tested.

## Paused

Until P1-P3 work end to end, do not extend intelligence dashboards, global maps, feeds, conferences, unrelated AI agents, additional public pages, subscriptions, native mobile apps, or visual redesigns that do not improve the marketplace flow.

## MVP definition of done

- Vendor verifies a company, completes target fit and required credentials, and publishes a standardized product or service.
- Buyer finds it through relevant filters and compares it with alternatives.
- Buyer contacts the vendor or asks an NXT Link advisor.
- A structured quote is stored and visible to authorized parties.
- A demo/pilot is scheduled, measured, and decided when required.
- Final purchase decision, transaction value, and fee obligation are recorded.
- Buyer, vendor, and operator see accurate status while private data remains isolated.
- Typecheck, core lint, unit tests, authorization/RLS tests, build, and one end-to-end persona test pass.
- Legal/fee/pilot language has professional review before real commerce.

## Weekly operating rhythm

Board: `Backlog -> This Sprint -> In Progress -> Needs Review -> Verified -> Done`.

- Maximum three items in progress.
- Every item names the persona, visible outcome, acceptance test, and owner.
- “Code exists” is not done; “verified with the correct persona” is done.
- New ideas go to backlog unless they fix an active P0-P3 blocker.
- Weekly report: shipped, verified, blocked, decisions needed, next three priorities.
