# NXT Link Industrial Marketplace Blueprint

Updated: 2026-07-08

This is the definitive product model for NXT Link. It clarifies and narrows
the earlier private-sourcing plan without discarding its confidentiality,
quote, pilot, agreement, and success-fee controls.

## 1. Product definition

NXT Link is a bilingual web application for industrial buyers, vendors, and
NXT Link operators.

Vendors list their companies, products, services, bundles, rentals, and pilot
programs in a standardized format. Buyers browse, search, filter, save, and
compare those offerings; request an estimate or firm quote; communicate with
the vendor; or ask an NXT Link advisor for help. Because industrial purchases
are complex, NXT Link supports demonstrations, site assessments, pilots,
measured results, final quotes, purchases, implementation, and support.

Positioning:

> Discover, compare, pilot, prove, and purchase industrial technology with
> less risk.

The marketplace has two valid buyer paths:

1. **Self-service:** browse -> compare -> contact vendor -> quote/demo -> pilot
   if needed -> purchase.
2. **NXT-assisted:** describe need -> advisor qualifies it -> matched shortlist
   -> comparable quotes -> demo/site visit -> measured pilot -> purchase.

NXT Link is not merely a directory and not ordinary consumer checkout. Its
core value is standardized industrial information, trustworthy credentials,
comparable total costs, pilot-before-purchase evidence, and accountable
coordination.

## 2. User portals

### Buyer / client

Buyers can:

- Create a personal account and company profile.
- Search by industry, problem, category, budget, location, timeline, company
  size, facility type, certification, compatibility, and buying method.
- Browse products, services, bundles, rentals, and pilot programs.
- Compare up to five offerings using the same standardized fields.
- Save listings and shortlists.
- Request an instant estimate when rules permit or a vendor-confirmed quote.
- Message a vendor, request a demo, schedule a call/site visit, or ask an NXT
  Link advisor.
- Approve what information is shared.
- Follow quote, demo, pilot, purchase, installation, training, and support
  status.
- Review a vendor only after a verified engagement.

### Vendor / service provider

Vendors can:

- Build a company storefront.
- Select a maturity type: startup/emerging, established vendor, service
  provider, manufacturer, distributor, integrator, consultant, or hybrid.
- Complete identity, credential, insurance, and evidence requirements.
- Define their ideal customer and exclusions.
- Create standardized product, service, bundle, rental, and pilot listings.
- Use AI to extract draft information from brochures, but approve every field.
- Receive and manage inquiries, messages, quote requests, demos, and pilots.
- Create and revise structured quotes.
- Track introductions, purchases, fees, and payouts when payments are enabled.

### NXT Link operator / admin

Operators can:

- Verify businesses, documents, credentials, and evidence.
- Approve, restrict, unpublish, suspend, and restore listings.
- Help buyers define needs and compare options.
- Match buyers to appropriate vendors without pay-to-win ranking.
- Coordinate quotes, demonstrations, site assessments, pilots, and purchases.
- Enforce confidentiality, agreements, introduction protection, and fee terms.
- Manage credential expiration, reports, disputes, refunds, and audit history.
- See action queues, stalled opportunities, and marketplace performance.

## 3. Marketplace listing types

Every vendor can create separate listings for:

- **Product** — physical equipment, software, or technology.
- **Service** — installation, integration, maintenance, consulting, training,
  programming, safety/compliance, logistics, rental, or financing support.
- **Bundle / complete solution** — product plus selected services.
- **Pilot program** — a defined test with duration, scope, price, and success
  criteria.
- **Rental / lease** — temporary access with terms and availability.

Products and services must be linkable so buyers can compare complete
solutions rather than sticker prices alone.

## 4. Vendor company and fit profile

Every vendor profile must capture:

- Legal company name, public name, entity location, website, and authorized
  representative.
- Vendor type and maturity stage.
- Products/services offered and service territory.
- Supported languages and emergency availability.
- Target industries and subindustries.
- Problems and use cases solved.
- Target company sizes by employees, revenue, facilities, or throughput.
- Target facility types and geographic regions.
- Typical minimum/maximum project budget and contract size.
- Typical timeline and implementation prerequisites.
- Technologies, equipment, and integrations supported.
- Certifications customers may require.
- Buyer roles normally involved.
- Customers and situations that are not a fit.

This **Vendor Fit Profile** powers search, matching, lead routing, and vendor
notifications. A vendor must complete it before receiving matched leads.

## 5. Credential tokens and permissions

Tokens are non-transferable marketplace permissions backed by evidence and an
expiration date. A token must say what NXT Link actually checked; it must not
imply that NXT Link guarantees the offering.

Core tokens:

- Business Identity Verified
- Authorized Representative Verified
- Insurance Verified
- Applicable Certifications Verified
- License Verified (when applicable)
- Customer Evidence Verified
- Product Information Complete
- Service Information Complete
- Pilot Ready
- Authorized Installer / Distributor (when claimed)
- Bilingual Service
- Marketplace Terms Accepted
- Fee Terms Accepted

Permission ladder:

| Status | Public company profile | Listings | Inquiries | On-site pilot | Purchase/payment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | No | Draft only | No | No | No |
| Startup / evidence developing | Yes, labeled | Limited discovery listings | Yes | Only after required safety/insurance approval | No automatic purchase |
| Marketplace ready | Yes | Publish complete listings | Yes | After pilot approval | Quote/purchase eligible |
| Pilot ready | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Quote/purchase eligible |
| Suspended / expired | Limited or hidden | Hidden/unpublished | Restricted | No | No |

Expiration of a required insurance, license, or certification automatically
removes the permission that depends on it. Category rules determine which
tokens are required; “not applicable” must be reviewed, not self-declared as
a shortcut.

## 6. Startup and emerging-technology policy

Startups are welcome, but maturity must be visible. They may publish a company
and discovery profile after identity verification, accurate product basics,
target-customer fit, marketplace terms, and disclosure that evidence is still
developing.

They must not claim verified performance without evidence. Instead of a case
study, a startup may supply independent testing, a customer reference, a
documented pilot, certification evidence, or a clearly labeled proposal for
an NXT-supervised pilot. On-site activity still requires the applicable safety
and insurance permissions.

## 7. Standardized product information

Every product listing uses the same blocks:

1. Name, category, overview, manufacturer, model, and version.
2. Industries, problems solved, use cases, and best-fit customers.
3. Technical specifications, capacity, dimensions, power, network, space,
   environmental, safety, and integration requirements.
4. Buying options: buy, rent, lease, subscription, or request quote.
5. Base price/range, quantity rules, options, accessories, and exclusions.
6. Availability, inventory status, lead time, shipping regions, and delivery.
7. Installation scope, prerequisites, downtime, timeline, and cost.
8. Training format, hours, seats, languages, included/extra cost.
9. Warranty duration, coverage, exclusions, response SLA, and extended options.
10. Support, maintenance schedule/cost, spare parts, upgrades, and end of life.
11. Certifications, testing, recalls, known risks, and mitigations.
12. Pilot availability, duration, price, scope, responsibilities, measurements,
    exit conditions, and whether pilot cost credits toward purchase.
13. Evidence: case studies, references, test reports, documents, and videos.
14. One-year and three-year estimated total cost of ownership.

Each material field is labeled **Vendor provided**, **NXT checked**, or **Not
confirmed**. Estimated, quoted, and final prices must never be presented as
the same thing.

## 8. Standardized service information

Every service listing includes:

- Service category, industries, problems solved, and deliverables.
- On-site, remote, or hybrid delivery.
- Geographic coverage and travel policy.
- Equipment, platforms, and technologies supported.
- Hourly, daily, fixed-project, milestone, or recurring pricing.
- Minimum engagement, typical duration, response time, and availability.
- Team size, experience, licenses, certifications, and insurance.
- Process, customer responsibilities, prerequisites, and exclusions.
- Workmanship guarantee, support, emergency service, and change-order policy.
- Case studies, references, and measurable outcomes.
- Target company size, facility type, budget, and situations that are not a fit.

## 9. Standard packages and comparison

Listings may expose four understandable packages:

- Product Only
- Ready to Deploy
- Turnkey Solution
- Pilot Before Purchase

The comparison experience normalizes:

- Product/service price
- Shipping and delivery
- Installation
- Integration
- Training
- Warranty and support
- Maintenance and consumables
- Lead/response time
- Certifications and credential status
- Pilot availability
- One-year and three-year total cost
- Missing or unconfirmed information

Search ranking prioritizes technical fit, total cost, budget fit, availability,
verified evidence, service coverage, support, and response performance.
Sponsored results are allowed only when clearly labeled and must not replace
minimum technical-fit requirements.

## 10. Quote, contact, and advisor choices

Every eligible listing provides:

- Get estimate / Request firm quote
- Message vendor
- Schedule call or demo
- Request site assessment
- Compare
- Ask an NXT Link advisor

An instant estimate is allowed only when the vendor has supplied deterministic
price rules. Complex configurations receive an immediate estimated range, not
a false “instant final quote.” Firm quotes include equipment/services,
quantity, options, shipping, installation, integration, training, warranty,
support, taxes, payment terms, delivery, assumptions, exclusions, and expiry.

Contact remains inside NXT Link so the buyer gains a useful record and NXT Link
can protect the introduction, coordinate work, and enforce platform rules.

## 11. Industrial pilot methodology

The standard buying journey is:

`Discover -> Compare -> Qualify -> Demo/Site Assessment -> Pilot Plan -> Pilot
Execution -> Results Review -> Final Quote -> Selection -> Implementation ->
Acceptance -> Support`

Every pilot records:

- Business problem and baseline.
- Scope, site, duration, equipment, and participants.
- Vendor, customer, and NXT Link responsibilities.
- Safety, insurance, access, data, and confidentiality requirements.
- Installation and training.
- Success metrics, measurement method, target, and data owner.
- Pilot price and whether it credits toward purchase.
- Stop conditions, damage/removal rules, and exit responsibilities.
- Results, customer decision, and approved case-study permissions.

## 12. Revenue and transaction protection

NXT Link earns a disclosed, versioned percentage on protected purchases and
may also charge a fixed pilot/advisor coordination fee. The final percentage,
fee base, exclusions, thresholds, and payer require legal/accounting approval.

The commercial record must define:

- Who pays the fee and when it is earned.
- Which taxes, shipping, refundable deposits, pass-through costs, refunds, and
  cancellations are excluded or adjusted.
- Introduction-protection period.
- Pilot-fee credit toward purchase.
- Evidence of final transaction value.
- Refund, dispute, warranty, and chargeback responsibility.

When marketplace payments are introduced, a regulated marketplace payment
provider routes funds, deducts NXT Link's fee, and pays the vendor. NXT Link
must not manually hold customer funds or call a service “escrow” without the
appropriate compliant provider and legal structure.

## 13. Required policies

Before accepting real pilots, purchases, or payments, NXT Link needs
attorney-reviewed versions of:

- Marketplace Terms
- Vendor Agreement and Listing Policy
- Buyer Terms
- Pilot Agreement
- Fee and Introduction-Protection Agreement
- Privacy and Cookie Policy
- Product Safety / Recall Policy
- Reviews and Case-Study Policy
- Refund, Dispute, and Suspension/Appeal Policy

Vendors remain responsible for product authenticity, lawful sale, safety,
claims, fulfillment, warranties, licenses, certifications, taxes, and
applicable reporting. NXT verification describes reviewed evidence; it is not
a product guarantee.

## 14. MVP boundary

The MVP must prove one category end to end:

1. Vendor verifies company and completes fit profile.
2. Vendor publishes one standardized product or service.
3. Buyer finds it through relevant filters and compares it.
4. Buyer contacts vendor or requests an advisor.
5. A structured quote is produced.
6. A demo/pilot is scheduled and measured when needed.
7. The final purchase decision and NXT Link fee obligation are recorded.
8. Every party can see status; private data remains isolated.

Payments, financing, advanced logistics, subscriptions, native mobile apps,
reviews, and broad industry expansion follow only after this flow succeeds.

## 15. Marketplace success metrics

- Verified and marketplace-ready vendors by category
- Complete published products/services by category
- Searches producing at least three relevant results
- Inquiry and quote response time
- Search -> compare -> inquiry conversion
- Inquiry -> demo/pilot conversion
- Pilot -> purchase conversion
- Total purchase value and NXT Link fee revenue
- Credential expiration and dispute rates
- Buyer repeat usage and vendor renewal

## 16. Deal-flow protection and anti-circumvention (definitive)

Owner rule (2026-07-08): **Public discovery is open; the deal flow is
protected.** NXT Link must be where the deal happens, not just where buyers and
vendors find each other. This section governs UI, API, and the Vendor
Agreement, and overrides any softer wording elsewhere.

**Open (no gate):** vendor names, company profiles, products, services,
brochures, and case studies are publicly browsable. NXT Link is not hiding the
marketplace.

**Protected (must run through NXT Link):** quotes, sales conversations, demos,
pilots, purchases, and commissions.

**Listing call-to-action rule.** Every eligible listing's PRIMARY actions must
be, and must be labeled as going through NXT Link:

- Request Quote
- Contact Sales
- Request Demo
- Request Pilot
- Ask a Question
- Compare Offers
- Accept Quote

The primary conversion path must NOT be "visit vendor website" or "email vendor
directly." A vendor's external website may appear later as secondary company
information only, never as the main action.

**Anti-circumvention logic (platform + Vendor Agreement):**

1. A request submitted through NXT Link is an NXT-originated lead.
2. The vendor must respond to it inside NXT Link.
3. The vendor may not redirect that buyer off-platform to avoid commission.
4. If the same opportunity closes off-platform within the protected period, the
   NXT Link commission is still owed.
5. The vendor must not add the commission on top as a buyer penalty; the NXT
   Link quote is a legitimate quote for the same scope.
6. Every request creates a tracked opportunity record: buyer, vendor, listing,
   date requested, scope, quote amount, status, commission terms, protected
   period, messages, documents, pilot/demo status, purchase status.
7. Before a vendor can receive quote requests they must accept NXT Link terms:
   commission owed on NXT-originated deals, no circumvention, protected lead
   period, quote-response rules, reporting closed deals, listing-accuracy
   responsibility, and NXT Link's right to suspend vendors who bypass the
   platform.
8. Before a buyer submits a request they must see a disclosure: the request is
   managed through NXT Link, NXT Link may receive a commission from the vendor,
   and the buyer may compare and communicate through the platform.

**Target flow:** buyer discovers openly -> requests through NXT Link -> vendor
responds through NXT Link -> commission and protected period tracked -> deal
status stays inside NXT Link.

## 17. Positioning and definitive build order (2026-07-08)

Positioning: **"Alibaba for industrial equipment, but cleaner, more organized,
and protected by NXT Link's quote and commission flow."** The product blends:

- Amazon-style browsing
- Alibaba-style RFQ
- Thomasnet-style supplier discovery
- GlobalSpec-style technical search
- NXT Link-controlled quote and commission tracking

Mantra: **do not build a leaky directory.** The website is where buyers
discover, compare, request quotes, contact sales, track pilots, and move deals
forward — not just find each other.

Owner-set build order (this supersedes earlier build-order lists when they
conflict):

1. Vendor agreement + revenue protection (accept terms before publish/leads)
2. Vendor structured listings (category-specific templates)
3. Marketplace search / filter (facets from real vendor data only)
4. Save & compare
5. Request quote (through NXT Link)
6. Vendor quote response (inside NXT Link)
7. Commission tracking (opportunity + commission record per quote request)
8. Demo / pilot tracking
9. Trust layer (verified vendor, insurance, certifications, warranty info,
   pilot available, case studies, report listing, verified reviews)
10. Innovation radar + analytics

Progress as of 2026-07-08: steps 3, 4, 5 built; 2 and 9 partial; steps 1, 6, 7,
8, 10 not yet built. Current focus: step 1.
