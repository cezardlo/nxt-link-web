# Plan 08 — Revenue Model

## Revenue Model

NXT//LINK monetizes a two-sided border-region marketplace where the demand side (El Paso warehouses/3PLs, Juárez maquiladoras and manufacturers) must stay nearly frictionless, and the supply side (vendors, service providers, consultants) pays for access to pre-qualified, quote-ready demand. Every model below is mapped to what the repo can already support.

**Billing status — cross-cutting:** there is **no payment infrastructure anywhere in the repo** (no Stripe, no invoicing tables). Every model below is therefore MISSING its collection layer even when its evidence/workflow layer is BUILT. Near-term workaround: manual invoicing via Zoho Mail (`src/lib/zoho/mail.ts`, logged in `zoho_outbox`); first billing build should be Stripe subscriptions + one-off invoices, with a `plan`/`featured` column added to `vendor_profiles` (currently absent). For Juárez-side customers, invoicing Mexican entities (USD vs MXN, CFDI/facturación requirements) needs verification with a cross-border accountant — flagged as an open item, not assumed.

**Phases used below:** **P0** pre-liquidity (no revenue by design) → **P1** first 50 vendors → **P2** post-event-1 → **P3** scale. All prices are **recommendations/estimates for a border-region B2B market**, not validated price points; SMB pricing here should sit well below big-metro SaaS norms.

### E. Recommended sequencing

1. **P0 — pre-liquidity: charge nothing on-platform.** Sign the introduction/commission acknowledgement on every routed opportunity anyway (`terms.ts` + `proof_of_introduction` — the paper trail must predate the pricing). Only consulting revenue, founder-delivered.
2. **P1 — first 50 vendors: turn on success-based + high-touch revenue.** Referral/implementation fees (first 3 intros per vendor commission-free), featured listings, premium matching, customer-paid technology audits. All invoiceable manually via Zoho; no billing build required yet.
3. **P2 — post-event-1: add events + recurring.** Sponsorships and booth fees fund event 1; launch vendor and service-provider subscriptions with grandfathered founding pricing; add managed procurement retainers, managed matchmaking, verification audits. **Build Stripe billing here.**
4. **P3 — scale: metered + media.** Pay-per-lead, pay-per-response overflow pricing, premium analytics as a standalone tier, verified-vendor advertising. Keep customer ticket submission free.

**Bilingual note (ES):** every priced surface must ship EN/ES like the existing `/intake`, `/vendor-signup`, and `/vendor/quotes` toggles — pricing page, invoices, commission terms (`terms.ts` already generates bilingual draft terms), and sponsorship decks. Recommend quoting prices in USD on both sides of the border with MXN shown as reference only (estimate; verify tax/invoicing treatment).

### F. 12-month revenue ramp logic (illustrative formula — not a forecast)

Monthly revenue ≈

```
R(m) = I(m)·f                        # referral fees: closed intros × avg fee
     + F(m)·p_f                      # featured listings: slots sold × monthly price
     + S(m)·p_s                      # subscriptions: paid vendors × blended ARPU
     + A(m)·p_a                      # audits/matching engagements × avg engagement price
     + E(m)·(k_sp·p_sp + k_b·p_b)    # events this month × (sponsors×tier + booths×fee)
     + M(m)·p_m                      # managed procurement/matchmaking retainers × monthly rate
where I(m) = T(m) · q · w            # tickets × quoted-rate × win-rate
```

Illustrative parameters (all assumptions): avg referral fee f=$750; featured $150/mo; blended subscription ARPU $99/mo; audit/matching engagement $1,200; event with 4 sponsors @ $3,000 + 15 booths @ $500; retainer $1,000/mo; q=60% of tickets get ≥1 quote, w=25% of quoted tickets close.

| Month | Tickets T | Intros closed I | Featured F | Paid subs S | Audits A | Events E | Retainers M | Illustrative R(m) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 (P1) | 12 | 2 | 3 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | ≈ $3.2k |
| 6 (P2, event 1) | 25 | 4 | 6 | 10 | 2 | 1 | 2 | ≈ $28.8k (≈$9.3k excl. event) |
| 9 | 40 | 6 | 8 | 25 | 3 | 0 | 4 | ≈ $15.9k |
| 12 | 60 | 9 | 10 | 45 | 4 | 1 | 6 | ≈ $45.8k (≈$26.3k excl. event) |

The structural takeaways survive any parameter change: referral fees and services carry months 1–6; events create step-function spikes and vendor-conversion moments; subscriptions become the compounding base only after vendors have won work; and the demand side stays free so T(m) — the input every other term multiplies — keeps growing.
