## 🔴 CONFLICTS — do NOT put these on the site (they contradict binding decisions + legal rules)

1. **"Capped 2% transaction fee / a $50,000 deal costs $200" — WRONG NUMBER.**
   The binding fee (DECISIONS-2026-07-21, enforced by the sacred fee engine,
   just re-verified in an Opus money review): **5% on the first $50,000, 3%
   above, capped at $20,000.** A $50,000 deal costs **$2,500**, not $200. The
   whole site was JUST corrected to 5%/3%/$20k (commit 5acad02) and Cesar
   approved that wording. Using "2% / $200" would re-break the pricing we just
   fixed and put a third wrong number on the site. If Cesar wants to CHANGE the
   fee to 2%/$200, that's a new pricing decision that must update the fee
   ENGINE + all copy together — not a marketing line. Until then: the number is
   5%/3%/$20k everywhere. **The "no membership fee / pay only when you close /
   capped fee" FRAMING is great and true — just with the correct number.**

2. **Escrow / "milestone-based escrow" / "escrow trust" / "escrow safety of
   Alibaba" / "hold funds" — BANNED.** Standing legal rule: NXT//LINK NEVER
   says or implies it holds/escrows funds (no Stripe/payments live yet). Points
   #1, #6, #9 lean heavily on escrow as a differentiator — that copy CANNOT
   ship. Substitute the trust we CAN truthfully claim: verified suppliers,
   protected introductions (12 months), contact masking until a quote is
   accepted, human vetting. No escrow, no "secure payments," no funds-holding.

3. **Features not yet real → no invented stats/claims.** "Performance metrics
   from actual transactions (on-time delivery, response rate)", "document-
   verified badges", milestone escrow pipeline — with ~6 vendors / ~23 listings
   / ~0 real closed deals, do NOT claim live metrics or capabilities that
   aren't shipped. Only claim what exists today; frame roadmap items as roadmap.

4. **Direct competitor $ comparisons** ("$3,000 Alibaba membership", "$4,000
   Thomasnet", "Amazon $39.99/mo + 12%") — comparative advertising with
   specific competitor prices needs substantiation + legal review before it
   goes on a public page. Keep the POSITIONING ("no membership fee, free to
   join") without asserting a competitor's exact prices as fact on-site.

## ✅ SAFE + STRONG — genuinely usable positioning (weave into About / landing / marketing, correct-numbers only)

- **Regional density (Borderplex / El Paso–Juárez):** true, real, and the core
  moat. "Find the best local suppliers, not 5,000 irrelevant global listings."
  Already reflected in the site ("Borderplex Industrial Marketplace"). Lean in.
- **Free to join, pay only when a deal closes, low capped fee** (with the REAL
  5%/3%/$20k number) — true and compelling risk-reversal.
- **Unified products + services** under one roof with a product/service toggle
  and standardized comparison — real, differentiating. Ties to the
  structured-search + filter-engine specs already filed.
- **Structured spec comparison** ("hide identical rows", best-value highlight)
  — real feature (QuoteCompare). Great buyer-value story.
- **Modern, ethical UX, no dark patterns** (no fake countdowns / "only 1 left" /
  spam) — true and on-brand; a real trust signal.
- **Easy 15-min guided vendor onboarding wizard** — real; the #1 supply-side
  unlock. Great for the vendor pitch.
- **Founding Vendor program** — real ($1,250 founding credit via manual flag).
  BUT frame per the decided policy: standard vendors get **$250 first-deal
  credit**; **$1,250 is the FOUNDING-vendor** tier (not "first several deals
  free" for everyone). Don't overstate.
- **Handles one-off sales AND multi-year contracts** (pilot → scale, recurring)
  — roadmap-ish; frame truthfully vs. what's shipped.
- **Human, honest brand voice** — "Post a Need" not "Submit RFQ", plain
  language, a real About page. Fully on-brand; already the direction. Adopt.
