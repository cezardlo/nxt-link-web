## 🔴 CONFLICTS — MUST NOT ship as written (legal + honesty rules)

1. **ESCROW / "Escrow Protected" / "Escrow Protection" / "$X in Secured Deals" —
   BANNED.** Standing legal rule: NXT//LINK NEVER says or implies it holds or
   escrows funds (no Stripe/payments live). The doc uses "Escrow Protected"
   (Block 2 trust strip, Block 3 step 3, Block 6, benchmark rows 5/9) — none of
   it can ship. Substitute the trust we CAN truthfully claim: **Verified
   vendors, Protected introductions (12 months), on-platform messaging &
   quotes, human vetting, local Borderplex focus.** No "escrow", no "secured
   deals", no "transact securely".

2. **INVENTED STATS — BANNED.** "1,200+ Verified Suppliers", "$4.2M+ in Secured
   Deals", "98% Dispute-Free", "Trusted by 5M+ businesses" are all fabricated —
   real inventory is ~6 vendors / ~23 listings / ~0 closed deals. Putting fake
   numbers on a B2B site is a credibility bomb (one real vendor screenshot ends
   trust) and legally risky. Use REAL small numbers or qualitative framing only.
   No stats bar with invented figures.

3. **CAMERA / NAMEPLATE IMAGE SEARCH — not built.** "camera icon for
   image/nameplate search", "killer app moment" — this feature does NOT exist.
   Cannot show a search control that doesn't work. Great ROADMAP idea; either
   omit for now or mark clearly "coming soon". Do NOT ship a dead camera button.

## ✅ SAFE + STRONG — adopt this (the structure is great)

- **Dual buyer/vendor entry, equally weighted** ("Find a Solution" / "Join as a
  Vendor") — already the direction (PublicHeader "Become a Vendor"). Keep/strengthen.
- **Benefit-driven hero** — "Find the right industrial supplier without searching
  the entire market." (calm, no outcome/escrow claims). Good — adopt (marketing
  final copy).
- **How-it-works 3-step strip** — Describe need → Get matched quotes → Compare &
  connect. (NOT "transact securely / escrow" — say "compare and connect".)
  Already have a timeline; align to this.
- **Industry-first browse (Block 4) + Products/Services toggle w/ category tiles
  (Block 5)** — industry as a BROWSE entry (tiles) is fine and distinct from the
  sidebar FILTER Cesar disliked. The new full-width category section is the seed.
- **Testimonials/case studies — REAL ONLY.** Rotating quotes only once real
  Borderplex buyers/vendors exist; until then, omit or use honest placeholder.
  No fake faces/quotes.
- **CTA repetition at bottom** (Find a Solution / Join as a Vendor) — good.
- **Clean footer** (About, Trust Center, How It Works, For Buyers, For Vendors,
  Terms, Privacy) — adopt; "Trust Center" must describe REAL protections only.
- **Calm black/white/violet premium aesthetic, generous whitespace, no dark
  patterns, real industrial photography (no fake stock)** — fully on-brand
  (Design System v1.0). Adopt.
