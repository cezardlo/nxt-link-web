01 — The brand

A bridge, not a directory.

NXT//LINK connects warehouses, manufacturers, logistics companies, and production facilities with trusted vendors for technology, equipment, products, and specialized services — worldwide.

Instead of searching hundreds of sites, businesses explain what they need, discover relevant vendors, compare options, and move projects forward through one connection point. The brand should feel modern, human, global, and industrial — professional without feeling rigid.

Brand attributes
Modern Human Global Industrial Intelligent Trustworthy Organized Approachable
The device

The double slash // is our owned mark — the point where a need links to a solution. It carries the brand alone, in the wordmark, and as a texture.

Clearspace & minimum size
NXT//LINK

Keep clearspace equal to the cap-height of “N” on all sides. Minimum wordmark width: 96px digital, 22mm print.

Don't
  • Recolor the slash to anything but Violet or its reversed tint.
  • Stretch, condense, outline, or add drop shadows.
  • Replace the “//” with other characters or spacing.
  • Place on busy imagery without an ink or white plate.
